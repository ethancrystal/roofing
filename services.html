How it goes

Five steps.
No surprises in any of them.

You'll always know what's happening next, who's coming, and what it costs. Here's the whole thing, start to finish.

01Free inspection
  1. 01

    The free inspection

    We come out, get on the roof, and photograph everything — including the parts you can't see. You get a written report with those photos whether or not you ever hire us. Takes about 45 minutes.

    45 minutes · No obligation
  2. 02

    A fixed price, in writing

    One number, broken down line by line, valid for 60 days. We'll walk you through what's urgent, what can wait a season, and what doesn't need doing at all — including when the honest answer is "your roof is fine."

    Within 48 hours · Valid 60 days
  3. 03

    We protect the house first

    Before a single shingle comes off: tarps over your landscaping, plywood against the siding, and your driveway kept clear. Tear-off happens in sections so your home is never left open overnight.

    Day one, before 8am
  4. 04

    The build

    The same four-person crew, every day, until it's done. Rotten decking gets replaced at cost — photographed and shown to you before it's covered. Most homes take two days.

    Typically 2 days
  5. 05

    We leave it cleaner than we found it

    Magnetic sweep of the lawn, driveway and flower beds — twice. Then a final walkaround with you, the photo record of the finished work, and your warranty paperwork in hand.

    Same day · 25-year warranty
