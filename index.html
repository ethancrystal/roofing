What we do

Everything above your ceiling.

Roofing, and the things that fail alongside it. One crew, one invoice, one warranty — instead of chasing three trades who each blame the other.

Full roof replacement

Complete tear-off down to the deck, rotten sheathing replaced at cost, new ice-and-water membrane, synthetic underlayment and your choice of architectural or designer shingle. Most homes are finished in two days.

  • Deck inspected and photographed before anything goes back on
  • 50-year manufacturer product warranty
  • 25-year Havenridge workmanship warranty

Leak repair & diagnosis

Water rarely enters where it shows up inside. We trace it properly — hose testing, moisture meter, attic inspection — so you're paying to fix the cause, not to patch a stain and hope.

  • Same-week appointments, emergency tarping within 24 hours
  • Written diagnosis with photos of the actual entry point
  • Flat $189 diagnostic, credited against the repair
  • Two-year warranty on every repair

Standing-seam metal

Concealed-fastener metal roofing that will very likely outlive your mortgage. Roll-formed on site to the exact length of your roof, so there are no horizontal seams for water to find.

  • 40–70 year realistic service life
  • Class 4 impact and Class A fire rated
  • Cuts summer attic temperatures noticeably
  • 24 standard colours, matched to your trim

Gutters & drainage

A perfect roof still fails if the water has nowhere to go. Seamless aluminium formed on your driveway, hidden hangers every 24 inches, and downspouts routed properly away from your foundation.

  • Seamless 5" and 6" K-style, 20 colours
  • Leaf guards that we'd actually put on our own homes
  • Fascia and soffit repair in the same visit
  • Annual clean-out plan available

Storm & insurance work

We document the damage the way adjusters need it documented, meet them on site, and handle the back-and-forth. You shouldn't have to become an expert in your own policy during a bad week.

  • Free storm assessment with a full photo report

Questions

The things people
actually ask us.

Can't see yours? Call us — it's a real person, 7am to 7pm, and we don't mind questions from people who aren't ready to buy.

How long does a roof replacement actually take?

Two days for most homes under 3,000 square feet. Complex roofs with multiple valleys, dormers or steep pitches run three to four. We'll tell you which yours is at the inspection, and we don't start a job we can't finish — your home is never left open overnight.

What happens if you find rot under the old roof?

We stop, photograph it, and call you before anything is covered back up. Replacement sheathing is charged at our cost — we don't mark up a problem you didn't know you had. On a typical home it's one or two sheets; the quote tells you what each one costs before we start.

Do I need to be home while you work?

No. Plenty of our customers are at work the whole time. You'll get photos at the end of each day, and the foreman's direct mobile number so you can reach a person rather than an office. We only need access to power and, ideally, a driveway.

Will my garden and driveway survive it?

This is the thing people worry about most, and fairly. Tarps go over beds and shrubs, plywood leans against the siding, and the dumpster sits on boards. We run a magnetic sweep of the lawn and driveway twice — once at the end of tear-off and again before we leave. If we miss a nail, we come back and find it.

Is it worth claiming on insurance?

Sometimes, and sometimes honestly not — a claim for less than your deductible isn't worth the premium increase. We'll photograph the damage properly, tell you what we think the claim is worth, and meet your adjuster on the roof. If it's not worth claiming, we'll say so.

Can you just repair it instead of replacing it?

Often, yes — and we'll tell you when that's the better call. A roof with eight good years left doesn't need replacing because someone knocked on your door and said so. Roughly a third of our inspections end with us telling someone their roof is fine.
